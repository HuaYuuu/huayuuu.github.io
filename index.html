About Me

I am an assistant researcher and postdoctoral researcher at the school of ocean engineering, Tsinghua Shenzhen International Graduate School (SIGS), working with Prof. You He (何友), Academician of the Chinese Academy of Engineering. I received my Ph.D. degree in control science and engineering from Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST) in 2025, advised by Prof. Luxin Yan (颜露新), and my bachelor's degree in logistics management (systems engineering track) from HUST in 2018.
